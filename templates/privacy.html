Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information you provide at registration (name, email address, password).
  • Expense records you create, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Basic usage data such as login timestamps and session activity.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to operate and improve the Service:

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session securely.
  • To store, display, and organise your expense records.
  • To generate summaries and category breakdowns shown within your account.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app performance.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond providing the Service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you choose to delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties. We use the following external services only for delivering the app itself:

  • Google Fonts — for typography (no personal data is shared).

No analytics platforms, ad networks, or data brokers have access to your information.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. You may also request an export of your expense data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us through the app. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
